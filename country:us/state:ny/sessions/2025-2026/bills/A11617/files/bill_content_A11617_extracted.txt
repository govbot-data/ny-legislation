Powers) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Agriculture AN ACT to amend the agriculture and markets law, in relation to exempt- ing the city of New York from certain regulations concerning frozen desserts The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Article 4-A of the agriculture and markets law is amended 2 by adding a new section 71-aa to read as follows: 3 § 71-aa. Exemption for certain cities. This article shall not apply to 4 a city with a population of one million or more. 5 § 2. This act shall take effect immediately.
