SUTTON -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the penal law, in relation to establishing the crime of private carrier package theft The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The penal law is amended by adding a new section 165.75 to 2 read as follows: 3 § 165.75 Private carrier package theft. 4 A person is guilty of private carrier package theft when such person 5 intentionally steals, takes or unlawfully possesses any package 6 entrusted to a private carrier, from the custody of a commercial carri- 7 er, including from a commercial carrier vehicle, or from any designated 8 delivery location including, but not limited to, a package locker, pack- 9 age room, porch, doorstep, doorway, vestibule or lobby, including any 10 such package that is in transit, that has been placed at the intended 11 delivery location, or that has been left for scheduled pickup by a 12 commercial carrier, before such package has been lawfully received by 13 the intended recipient or an authorized recipient of such package. 14 Private carrier package theft is a class E felony punishable by a fine 15 of not more than two hundred fifty thousand dollars. 16 § 2. Section 80.00 of the penal law is amended by adding a new subdi- 17 vision 8 to read as follows: 18 8. Notwithstanding any inconsistent provision of subdivision one of 19 this section, the court may impose a fine for a violation of section 20 165.75 of this chapter in an amount not to exceed two hundred fifty 21 thousand dollars. 22 § 3. This act shall take effect on the first of November next succeed- 23 ing the date on which it shall have become a law.
