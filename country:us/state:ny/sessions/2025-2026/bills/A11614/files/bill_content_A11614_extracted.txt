Santabar- bara) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Codes AN ACT to amend the executive law, in relation to establishing the New York state take me home program The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The executive law is amended by adding a new section 2 837-f-3 to read as follows: 3 § 837-f-3. New York state take me home program. 1. There is hereby 4 established within the division the New York state take me home program. 5 2. The division shall create a secure, voluntary statewide emergency 6 information registry to allow participants including, but not limited 7 to, parents, legal guardians and caregivers to provide information about 8 individuals who may be at increased risk of wandering, becoming lost or 9 having difficulty communicating during an emergency, before an emergency 10 occurs. Participants may voluntarily provide information to such regis- 11 try that may assist first responders to locate and assist such individ- 12 uals in the case of an emergency, including, but not limited to, the 13 following information about such individuals: 14 (a) recent photographs; 15 (b) physical descriptions; 16 (c) emergency contact information; 17 (d) communication methods or limitations; 18 (e) medical considerations; 19 (f) behavioral characteristics; 20 (g) sensory sensitivities; 21 (h) information regarding a history of wandering or becoming lost; 22 (i) places frequently visited; and 23 (j) other information that may assist first responders during an emer- 24 gency. 25 3. Information provided to such registry shall be accessible only to 26 authorized law enforcement personnel, emergency dispatchers and other 27 authorized first responders responding to an emergency or missing person 28 investigation.
