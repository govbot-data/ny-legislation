Wolfe was born on March 29, 1941, to Richard J. and Thelma Lezette Wolfe in Hudson, New York; and WHEREAS, A lifelong resident of Columbia County, Richard H. Wolfe graduated from Oakway High School in 1959; and WHEREAS, Shortly after graduating high school, Richard H. Wolfe enlisted in the United States Navy on July 22, 1959, and he attended basic training at Great Lakes Naval Station before receiving advanced training as a diesel motor mechanic at the Naval Training Center in Illinois; and WHEREAS, Upon completion of his training, Richard H. Wolfe was qualified to serve on submarines; between 1960 and 1963, Richard H. Wolfe was assigned to serve on the USS Bang as a machinist mate second class; and WHEREAS, During Richard H. Wolfe's service, the USS Bang, a 1526-ton Balao class submarine stationed out of the United States Naval Submarine Base in New London, Connecticut, operated in the Atlantic on operations and training exercises as a part of the national fleet readiness during the Cold War; and WHEREAS, Richard H. Wolfe was honorably discharged on July 17, 1963; for his courageous service aboard the USS Bang, Richard H. Wolfe was awarded the Good Conduct Medal; and WHEREAS, On March 25, 1961, Richard H. Wolfe married his beloved wife, Suzanne Surprenant, and together they shared the joy of raising their three sons, Richard L., Robert G., and Brian J. Wolfe; and WHEREAS, Upon returning home, Richard H. Wolfe worked as a truck driver for Philmont Fuel Supply and Harder's Express in Claverack, New York, until his retirement in the 1990s; and WHEREAS, Outside of work, Richard H. Wolfe remained very involved in his community; he was a proud member of the Melleville Volunteer Fire Company, Philmont Rescue Squad, Philmont Rotary Club, Philmont Methodist Church, Philmont American Legion, and Berkshire Hillsmen Barber Shop Chorus, in addition to serving as an election day poll site custodian in Chatham and Philmont; and WHEREAS, Richard H. Wolfe died on December 27, 2022, at the age of 81; and WHEREAS, Residents of this great State must never forget the courage with which these men and women served their country, and must recognize that no greater debt is owed than that owed to those who gave their lives for their beloved Nation and to those who continue to be missing in action; and WHEREAS, Having exhibited his patriotism both at home and abroad, Richard H. Wolfe demonstrated his love for his country and merits forevermore the highest respect of his State and Nation; and WHEREAS, Our Nation's veterans deserve to be recognized, commended, and thanked by the people of the State of New York for their service and for their dedication to their communities, their State, and their Nation; now, therefore, be it RESOLVED, That this Legislative Body pause in its deliberations to commend Richard Henry Wolfe posthumously upon the occasion of his designation as recipient of a Liberty Medal, the highest honor bestowed upon an individual by the New York State Senate; and be it further RESOLVED, That a copy of this Resolution, suitably engrossed, be transmitted to the family of Richard Henry Wolfe.
