ROLISON -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the education law, in relation to enacting Firefighter Joseph P. Spor Jr.'s Law requiring the establishment of September eleventh awareness curriculum or instruction for school districts The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Short title. This act shall be known and may be cited as 2 "Firefighter Joseph P. Spor Jr.'s Law". 3 § 2. The education law is amended by adding a new section 820 to read 4 as follows: 5 § 820. September eleventh awareness curriculum or instruction. 1. 6 Within one year of the effective date of this section, the commissioner, 7 in consultation with any appropriate agency, shall establish September 8 eleventh (9/11) awareness curriculum or instruction for school districts 9 regarding the September eleventh, two thousand one attacks on the United 10 States of America and their ongoing repercussions. The content of such 11 curriculum or instruction may be varied to meet the needs of particular 12 school districts, or portions thereof, and need not be uniform through- 13 out the state. 14 2. The content of such curriculum or instruction shall include the 15 following core tenets and themes: 16 (a) the historical context of terrorism; 17 (b) the terrorist attacks on the World Trade Center, the Pentagon and 18 United Airlines Flight 93 which crashed in Shanksville, Pennsylvania; 19 (c) American recovery and response to the attacks; 20 (d) global challenges and consequences in a post-9/11 world; 21 (e) examples of heroism, resilience, and service that emerged during 22 and after 9/11 from people all over the world; and 23 (f) the global response to terrorism following the events of 9/11. 24 3. The department shall make available on its website curriculum mate- 25 rials and other available resources regarding the events and signif- 26 icance of September eleventh, two thousand one, for voluntary use by 27 school entities.
