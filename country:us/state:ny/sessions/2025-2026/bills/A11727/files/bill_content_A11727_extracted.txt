Steck) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Alcoholism and Drug Abuse AN ACT to amend the mental hygiene law, in relation to the review and approval of information concerning the statewide opioid settlement agreement The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Subdivision (c-1) of section 25.18 of the mental hygiene 2 law, as added by section 1 of part II of chapter 57 of the laws of 2025, 3 is amended to read as follows: 4 (c-1) On or before November first of each year, any New York [subdivi- 5 sion] government entity that directly received funds pursuant to a 6 statewide opioid settlement agreement shall publicly post on their 7 website information regarding how such funding was utilized and shall 8 submit such information to the office of addiction services and 9 supports. Such information shall be updated on an annual basis. The 10 office of addiction services and supports shall review and approve such 11 information to ensure that the funding was utilized for purposes which 12 are consistent with this section, section ninety-nine-nn of the state 13 finance law and the statewide opioid settlement agreements and then 14 re-post such information, including their review and approval, on its 15 website. 16 § 2. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
