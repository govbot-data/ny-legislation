Add §§1229-e & 2292, amd §§2282 & 2283, V & T L
 
Enacts the "motorized recreational vehicle identification and accountability act" in relation to requiring the registration and visible identification of certain motorized recreational and personal mobility vehicles.

        AN ACT to amend the vehicle and traffic law, in  relation  to  requiring
          the  registration  and  visible  identification  of  certain motorized
          recreational and personal mobility vehicles

          The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and  Assem-
        bly, do enact as follows:
 
     1    Section  1.  Short  title. This act shall be known and may be cited as
     2  the "motorized recreational vehicle  identification  and  accountability
     3  act".
     4    §  2.  The  vehicle and traffic law is amended by adding a new section
     5  1229-e to read as follows:
     6    § 1229-e. Registration of certain motorized recreational and  personal
     7  mobility  vehicles  for  identification purposes. 1. For the purposes of
     8  this section, the term  "covered  motorized  recreational  and  personal
     9  mobility  vehicle" or "covered vehicle" shall include off-highway motor-
    10  cycles, dirt bikes, motorized minibikes and high-powered electric  vehi-
    11  cles that are not bicycles with electric assist.
    12    2.  All  covered motorized recreational and personal mobility vehicles
    13  shall be registered with the department at the point of sale  and  shall
    14  be  issued  an  identification  plate  or  tag  which  shall  be visibly
    15  displayed on such covered vehicle.
    16    3. Any dealer or other  commercial  seller  of  any  covered  vehicle,
    17  including  online  sellers delivering covered vehicles into the state of
    18  New York, shall submit covered vehicle and purchaser information to  the
    19  department  for  purposes  of  the registration required by this section
    20  prior to releasing a covered vehicle to a purchaser. Any private sale or
    21  other subsequent transfer of a covered vehicle shall also be reported to
    22  the department in order for the department to maintain a current  owner-
    23  ship record of such covered vehicle.
    24    4.  No  person shall remove, conceal, alter or fraudulently display an
    25  identification plate or tag required pursuant to this  section,  vehicle
    26  identification  number  or  serial  number  of  any covered vehicle. Law
    27  enforcement shall be authorized to impound any covered vehicle  operated

     2  with altered or fraudulent identification.
     3    5.  Registration of covered vehicles pursuant to this section shall be
     4  only for identification and accountability purposes.  Such  registration
     5  shall  not  authorize the operation of any covered vehicle on any public
     6  road or in any other location where such operation is otherwise  prohib-
     7  ited.
     8    6. The identification of a covered vehicle registered pursuant to this

    20    § 3. Subdivision 6-a of section 2282 of the vehicle and  traffic  law,
    21  as  added  by  section 1 of part D of chapter 59 of the laws of 2005, is
    22  amended to read as follows:
    23    6-a. Registration at time of sale. Every all terrain  vehicle  defined
    24  by section twenty-two hundred eighty-one of this article sold by a deal-
    25  er  or  other  commercial  seller  of all terrain vehicles, including an
    26  online seller delivering all terrain vehicles  into  the  state  of  New
    27  York,  shall  be registered at the time of sale of such vehicle prior to
    28  being released to the purchaser of such vehicle. Such registration shall
    29  be valid until the thirty-first day of August following the date of such
    30  sale; provided, however, that any all terrain vehicle sold  after  April
    31  first  of each year shall be issued a registration valid until the thir-
    32  ty-first day of August in the year  following  that  in  which  the  all
    33  terrain  vehicle  is  sold.  Any  all  terrain vehicle purchased for use
    34  exclusively outside of the state of New York shall not require registra-
    35  tion at the time of purchase, and the  purchaser  of  such  all  terrain
    36  vehicle  shall  sign  a declaration, provided by the dealer, which shall
    37  state that such purchaser understands the conditions under which an  all
    38  terrain vehicle must be registered and the penalty for violation of such
    39  registration  provisions.  Each signed declaration shall be forwarded by
    40  the dealer to the commissioner. The form of such  declaration  shall  be
    41  provided  by the commissioner to each dealer.  Any private sale or other
    42  subsequent transfer of an all terrain vehicle shall also be reported  to
    43  the  department in order for the department to maintain a current owner-
    44  ship record of such all terrain vehicle.
    45    § 4. The vehicle and traffic law is amended by adding  a  new  section
    46  2292 to read as follows:
    47    § 2292. Identification of illegally operated all terrain vehicle.  The
    48  identification  of  an  all  terrain  vehicle registered pursuant to the
    49  provisions of this article by its registration number as an all  terrain
    50  vehicle  operated illegally on a public street, sidewalk or other public
    51  property shall not be sufficient evidence to establish that  the  regis-
    52  tered  owner  of such all terrain vehicle was operating such all terrain
    53  vehicle. Such owner shall be provided notice that such all terrain vehi-
    54  cle was identified being operated illegally on a public street, sidewalk
    55  or other public property and an opportunity for a hearing  to  establish
