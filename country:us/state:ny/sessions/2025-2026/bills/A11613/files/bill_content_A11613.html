Add §390-p, Soc Serv L
 
Relates to regulating private equity in child care; requires certain child care providers to report certain information to the office of children and family services; establishes additional reporting requirements for child care providers that participate in the New York state child care assistance program or receive certain public funding, and for child care providers that include a significant equity investor; establishes certain conditions on the use of public funds received by child care providers; establishes statewide reporting requirements related thereto.
