Powers) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Election Law AN ACT to amend the election law, in relation to prohibiting certain election officials from being in political action committees and inde- pendent expenditure committees The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Section 3-100 of the election law is amended by adding a 2 new subdivision 6 to read as follows: 3 6. No commissioner shall serve as treasurer of, or have operational 4 control or managerial influence over, a political action committee or 5 independent expenditure committee for a candidate in an election such 6 commissioner is overseeing. For the purposes of this subdivision, the 7 following terms shall have the following meanings: 8 (a) "Independent expenditure committee" shall have the same meaning as 9 such term is defined in section 14-100 of this chapter. 10 (b) "Political action committee" shall have the same meaning as such 11 term is defined in section 14-100 of this chapter. 12 § 2. Section 3-200 of the election law is amended by adding a new 13 subdivision 8 to read as follows: 14 8. No board member shall serve as treasurer of, or have operational 15 control or managerial influence over, a political action committee or 16 independent expenditure committee for a candidate in an election such 17 board member is overseeing. For the purposes of this subdivision, the 18 following terms shall have the following meanings: 19 (a) "Independent expenditure committee" shall have the same meaning as 20 such term is defined in section 14-100 of this chapter. 21 (b) "Political action committee" shall have the same meaning as such 22 term is defined in section 14-100 of this chapter. 23 § 3. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
