Rosenthal) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Housing AN ACT to amend the real property law, in relation to requiring the disclosure of digitally altered images in an advertisement or other promotional material for the sale or lease of real property The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The real property law is amended by adding a new section 2 443-b to read as follows: 3 § 443-b. Disclosure of digitally altered images. 1. For purposes of 4 this section: 5 (a) "Digitally altered image" means a still-photo image, created by or 6 at the behest of the real estate broker, seller, property owner or prop- 7 erty manager, that has been altered through the use of photo editing 8 software or artificial intelligence to add, remove, or change elements 9 of the real property offered for sale or lease in the image, including, 10 but not limited to, furniture, appliances, flooring, and walls. 11 "Digitally altered image" does not include the use of photo editing 12 software to adjust lighting, sharpening, white balance, color 13 correction, angle, straightening, cropping, exposure, or other common 14 photo editing adjustments that do not change the conditions of the real 15 property. 16 (b) "AI-generated image" means a still-photo or video image created in 17 whole or in part by generative artificial intelligence, which, given a 18 set of human defined objectives, produces visual outputs. 19 2. (a) A real estate broker, seller, property owner or property manag- 20 er who includes a digitally altered or AI-generated image in an adver- 21 tisement or other promotional material for the sale or lease of real 22 property shall include in the advertisement or promotional material a 23 statement disclosing that the image has been altered. The statement 24 shall be conspicuously located on or adjacent to each altered image. 25 (b) If the advertisement or promotional material is posted on an 26 internet website, the real estate broker, seller, property owner or 27 property manager shall include the unaltered version of the images for EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
