HARCKHAM -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the real property tax law, in relation to the taxation of state lands in the county of Westchester The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Subdivision (d) of section 532 of the real property tax 2 law, as amended by chapter 364 of the laws of 2004, is amended and a new 3 subdivision (m) is added to read as follows: 4 (d) All lands in the counties of Rockland, Westchester and Putnam 5 acquired for a public use by the state, exclusive of the improvements 6 erected thereon by the state; 7 (m) All lands in the county of Westchester purchased or acquired by 8 the department of corrections pursuant to section twenty-one of the 9 correction law, exclusive of the improvements erected thereon by the 10 state. 11 § 2. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
