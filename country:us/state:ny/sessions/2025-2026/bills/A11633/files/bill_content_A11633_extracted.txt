Subdivision 3 of section 1203-a of the vehicle and traffic 2 law, as amended by chapter 220 of the laws of 1984, is amended to read 3 as follows: 4 3. (a) Notwithstanding any provision of this chapter to the contrary, 5 any municipality may issue a temporary special vehicle identification 6 parking permit to any person who is temporarily unable to ambulate with- 7 out the aid of an assisting device, as certified by a physician. Such 8 temporary special vehicle identification parking permit shall be valid 9 for not more than six months and shall be recognized statewide. 10 (b) Any resident of New York state who is seventy-five years of age or 11 older and who was issued a temporary special vehicle identification 12 parking permit pursuant to paragraph (a) of this subdivision shall be 13 eligible to apply for and shall be issued a permit pursuant to subdivi- 14 sion one of this section, as though such person was a severely disabled 15 person, as defined in subdivision four of section four hundred 16 four-a of this chapter, without providing updated medical certification 17 or additional medical documents upon application for renewal or issuance 18 of the permit. 19 § 1-a.
