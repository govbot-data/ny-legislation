Subdivision 6 of section 6438-c of the education law is 2 amended by adding a new paragraph (e) to read as follows: 3 (e) A student-athlete shall not enter into a contract providing 4 compensation for the use of the athlete's name, image, or likeness for 5 the endorsement of tobacco, alcohol, illegal substances or activities, 6 banned athletic substances, or any form of gambling or gaming, including 7 sports wagering. 8 § 2.
