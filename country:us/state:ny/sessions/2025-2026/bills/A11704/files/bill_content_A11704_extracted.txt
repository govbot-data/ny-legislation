Yeger) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Local Governments AN ACT to amend the general municipal law and the administrative code of the city of New York, in relation to prohibiting the use of adhesive stickers in the enforcement of street parking rules; and to repeal certain provisions of the administrative code of the city of New York relating thereto The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The general municipal law is amended by adding a new 2 section 99-aa to read as follows: 3 § 99-aa. Adhesive stickers in the enforcement of street parking rules 4 prohibited. It shall be unlawful for any department of a municipality 5 tasked with parking enforcement to affix an adhesive sticker to any part 6 of a motor vehicle solely in connection with enforcement of street park- 7 ing rules. 8 § 2. Section 19-163.2 of the administrative code of the city of New 9 York is REPEALED and a new section 19-163.2 is added to read as follows: 10 § 19-163.2 Adhesive stickers in the enforcement of street parking 11 rules prohibited. It shall be unlawful for any department to affix an 12 adhesive sticker to any part of a motor vehicle solely in connection 13 with enforcement of street parking rules. 14 § 3. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
