Add §305-a, Ed L
 
Prohibits humanoid robots and AI systems from being used in schools; provides limited exceptions; provides for student privacy protections; makes related provisions.

        Introduced  by  COMMITTEE  ON RULES -- (at request of M. of A. Tapia) --
          read once and referred to the Committee on Education
 
        AN ACT to amend the education law, in relation to human-led  instruction
          and artificial intelligence in schools
 
          The  People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem-
        bly, do enact as follows:

     1    Section 1. The education law is amended by adding a new section  305-a
     2  to read as follows:
     3    § 305-a. Human-led instruction and artificial intelligence in schools.
     4  1.  Definitions. For purposes of this section, the following terms shall
     5  have the following meanings:
     6    (a) "Artificial intelligence system" means a machine-based system that
     7  can, for a given set  of  human-defined  objectives,  make  predictions,
     8  recommendations,  or decisions influencing real or virtual environments,
     9  and that uses machine- and  human-based  inputs  to  perceive  real  and
    10  virtual  environments,  abstract  such  perceptions  into models through
    11  analysis in an automated manner, and use model  inference  to  formulate
    12  options for information or action.
    13    (b) "Humanoid robot" means any programmable robotic device designed to
    14  physically  resemble a human being and capable of autonomous or semi-au-
    15  tonomous verbal or physical interaction with students.
    16    (c) "Instructional setting" means any classroom, laboratory,  instruc-
    17  tional  space,  library,  learning  center,  virtual classroom, or other
    18  location where  educational  instruction  or  school-sponsored  academic
    19  activities are provided to students.
    20    2.  Prohibition on humanoid robots. No school district, board of coop-
    21  erative educational services, charter school, nonpublic school,  or  any
    22  employee,  contractor,  or  agent thereof shall purchase, lease, deploy,
    23  employ, contract for, or otherwise utilize a humanoid  robot  within  an
    24  instructional setting. A humanoid robot shall not:
    25    (a) deliver instruction;
    26    (b) facilitate classroom activities;
    27    (c) answer instructional questions;
    28    (d) supervise students;

     1    (e) assess or evaluate student performance;
     2    (f) provide behavioral or disciplinary supervision;
     3    (g) provide counseling or social-emotional support; or
     4    (h) otherwise interact with students in an instructional setting.
     5    3.  Limited  exceptions. (a) The provisions of subdivision two of this
     6  section shall not prohibit the temporary  presence  or  operation  of  a
     7  humanoid robot solely:
     8    (i)  as  part  of a course of study in robotics, engineering, computer
     9  science, artificial intelligence systems, or substantially similar tech-
    10  nical education;
    11    (ii) during scientific demonstrations, exhibitions,  museum  programs,
    12  or research activities; or
    13    (iii) for maintenance, testing, repair, or transportation unrelated to
    14  classroom instruction.
    15    (b)  Nothing  contained  in  this  subdivision  shall  be construed to
    16  authorize a humanoid robot to perform  any  instructional,  supervisory,
    17  counseling, evaluative, or classroom management function.
    18    4. Human-led instruction. (a) No school district, board of cooperative
    19  educational  services, charter school, or nonpublic school shall utilize
    20  an artificial intelligence system, chatbot, avatar,  virtual  assistant,
    21  or  substantially  similar  technology  as the primary instructor in any
    22  instructional setting.
    23    (b) All instructional settings shall remain under  the  direction  and
    24  control of a certified educator or other appropriately authorized school
    25  employee.
    26    (c)  Artificial intelligence systems may be utilized solely as supple-
    27  mental instructional resources under the direct supervision of a  certi-
    28  fied educator.
    29    5.  Student privacy protections. (a) No artificial intelligence system
    30  utilized  by  a  school  district,  board  of  cooperative   educational
    31  services,  charter  school,  or  nonpublic school shall collect, retain,
    32  sell, license, disclose, transfer, or otherwise use:
    33    (i) biometric identifiers or biometric information;
    34    (ii) facial recognition data;
    35    (iii) voiceprints;
    36    (iv) iris scans;
    37    (v) retinal scans;
    38    (vi) fingerprints;
    39    (vii) gait recognition data;
    40    (viii) emotional recognition, affect recognition, or emotion inference
    41  data; or
    42    (ix) personally identifiable student information for  the  purpose  of
    43  training,  improving, testing, or developing any artificial intelligence
    44  model or for any commercial purpose.
    45    (b) Student prompts, conversations, audio recordings, written communi-
    46  cations, or other interactions with an  artificial  intelligence  system
    47  shall  not  be retained following completion of an instructional session
    48  and shall not be used  for  advertising,  profiling,  commercialization,
    49  artificial  intelligence model training, or any purpose unrelated to the
    50  immediate instructional setting.
    51    (c) No school district, board  of  cooperative  educational  services,
    52  charter  school,  or nonpublic school shall require a student to consent
    53  to the collection of information prohibited by  this  subdivision  as  a
    54  condition of participating in any educational program or activity.
    55    6.  Annual  certification.  Each school district, board of cooperative
    56  educational services, charter school,  and  nonpublic  school  utilizing
