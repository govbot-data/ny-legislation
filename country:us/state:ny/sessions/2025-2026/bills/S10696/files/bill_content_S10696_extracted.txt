FAHY -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the penal law, in relation to the crime of promoting an obscene sexual performance by a child The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Section 263.00 of the penal law is amended by adding a new 2 subdivision 10 to read as follows: 3 10. "Obscene synthetic sexual performance" means any performance 4 created through artificial intelligence or other digital or computa- 5 tional means that realistically depicts a person who reasonably appears 6 to be a child less than seventeen years of age engaging in sexual 7 conduct, regardless of whether such depicted child corresponds to an 8 actual person. 9 § 2. Section 263.10 of the penal law, as amended by section 1 of part 10 L of chapter 55 of the laws of 2025, is amended to read as follows: 11 § 263.10 Promoting an obscene sexual performance by a child. 12 A person is guilty of promoting an obscene sexual performance by a 13 child when, knowing the character and content thereof, such person 14 produces, directs or promotes any obscene sexual performance which 15 includes sexual conduct by a child less than seventeen years of age, 16 including a performance created or altered by digitization as defined in 17 section 245.15 of this part, or when, knowing the character and content 18 thereof, such person produces, directs or promotes any obscene synthetic 19 sexual performance. 20 Promoting an obscene sexual performance by a child is a class D felo- 21 ny. 22 § 3. Severability. If any clause, sentence, paragraph, subdivision, 23 section or part of this act shall be adjudged by any court of competent 24 jurisdiction to be invalid, such judgment shall not affect, impair or 25 invalidate the remainder thereof, but shall be confined in its operation 26 to the clause, sentence, paragraph, subdivision, section or part thereof 27 directly involved in the controversy in which such judgment shall have 28 been rendered. 29 § 4. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
