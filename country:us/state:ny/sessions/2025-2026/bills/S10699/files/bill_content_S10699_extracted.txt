LANZA -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the vehicle and traffic law, in relation to establishing distinctive "In God We Trust" license plates The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The vehicle and traffic law is amended by adding a new 2 section 404-ff to read as follows: 3 § 404-ff. Distinctive "In God We Trust" license plates. 1. Any person 4 residing in this state shall, upon request, be issued a license plate 5 bearing the words "In God We Trust". Application for said license plate 6 shall be filed with the commissioner in such form and detail as the 7 commissioner shall prescribe. 8 2. A distinctive plate issued pursuant to this section shall be issued 9 in the same manner as other number plates upon the payment of the regu- 10 lar registration fee prescribed in section four hundred one of this 11 article, provided, however, that an additional annual service charge of 12 twenty-five dollars shall be charged for such plate. 13 § 2. 1. A distinctive plate established pursuant to section 404-ff of 14 the vehicle and traffic law, as added by section one of this act, shall 15 only be designed, produced and issued upon the delivery to the depart- 16 ment of motor vehicles of a surety bond in the amount of six thousand 17 dollars, which shall be executed by a surety company authorized by the 18 department of financial services to transact business in this state. 19 Provided, however, that if the commissioner of motor vehicles shall have 20 received prior to plate design, production and issuance at least two 21 hundred orders for such distinctive plate together with the additional 22 annual service charge applicable to each such order, which shall be 23 non-refundable, no such surety bond shall be required. All service 24 charges collected pursuant to this section shall be deposited pursuant 25 to the provisions of section 404-oo of the vehicle and traffic law to 26 the credit of the department of motor vehicles distinctive plate devel- 27 opment fund established by section 95-g of the state finance law and EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
