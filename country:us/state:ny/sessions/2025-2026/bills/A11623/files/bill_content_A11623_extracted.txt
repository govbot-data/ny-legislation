Section 4903 of the public health law is amended by adding 2 a new subdivision 10 to read as follows: 3 10. The commissioner, in conjunction with the superintendent of finan- 4 cial services, shall develop standards for prior authorization requests 5 to be utilized by all health care plans for the purpose of submitting a 6 request for a utilization review determination for coverage of health 7 care services under this chapter. The department and the department of 8 financial services, in development of the standards, shall require an 9 electronic prior authorization program and shall take into consideration 10 existing electronic prior authorization standards. 11 § 2. Section 4903 of the insurance law is amended by adding a new 12 subsection (j) to read as follows: 13 (j) The superintendent, in conjunction with the commissioner of 14 health, shall develop standards for prior authorization requests to be 15 utilized by all health care plans for the purpose of submitting a 16 request for a utilization review determination for coverage of health 17 care services under this chapter. The department and the department of 18 health, in development of the standards, shall require an electronic 19 prior authorization program and shall take into consideration existing 20 electronic prior authorization standards. 21 § 3. This act shall take effect immediately. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
