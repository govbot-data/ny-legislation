Amd §13-g, Work Comp L
 
Imposes an increased percentage of interest from 1.5% to 3%, or up to 5% for a pattern or practice of non-payment, and other civil penalties on certain employers or their workers compensation insurance carriers for non-payment of a bill for medical care or supplies.

    26  carrier's liability, or for a self-insured employer that is  responsible
