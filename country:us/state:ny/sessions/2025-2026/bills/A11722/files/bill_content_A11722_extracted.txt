The public health law is amended by adding a new section 2 2833 to read as follows: 3 § 2833. Provision of diapers, wipes and formula to parents and legal 4 guardians following childbirth. 1. Every hospital licensed pursuant to 5 this article that provides maternity services shall, upon the discharge 6 of a newborn following childbirth, provide the parents or legal guardian 7 of such newborn with the following: 8 (a) a supply of disposable diapers and wipes for the newborn, in an 9 amount to be determined by the department; 10 (b) a supply of menstrual products, in an amount to be determined by 11 the department; and 12 (c) the option to receive a one-week supply of infant formula, at no 13 cost, for the newborn. 14 2. Acceptance or refusal of such supplies shall be voluntary and at 15 the sole discretion of the parents or legal guardian. 16 3. Compliance with the provisions of this section shall not affect a 17 hospital's eligibility to participate in the department's breastfeeding 18 friendly practice designation program. 19 4. The commissioner shall promulgate rules and regulations necessary 20 to implement the provisions of this section, including but not limited 21 to: 22 (a) establishing the minimum quantity of disposable diapers, wipes, 23 and menstrual products required to be provided pursuant to paragraphs 24 (a) and (b) of subdivision one of this section; and EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
