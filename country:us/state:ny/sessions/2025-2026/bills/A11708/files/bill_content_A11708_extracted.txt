Rosenthal) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Election Law AN ACT to amend the election law, in relation to providing notice of polling place location changes The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Paragraph (a) of subdivision 2 of section 4-104 of the 2 election law, as amended by chapter 603 of the laws of 2025, is amended 3 and a new paragraph (c) is added to read as follows: 4 (a) If the board of elections, after designating a polling place, and 5 after sending written notice of such polling place to each registered 6 voter, designates an alternative polling place, it must, at least [five] 7 sixty days before the next election or day for registration, send by 8 mail a written notice to each registered voter notifying such voter of 9 the changed location of such polling place. If such notice is not possi- 10 ble the board of elections must provide for an alternative form of 11 notice to be given to voters at the location of the previous polling 12 place. The board of elections shall publish such notice of the changed 13 location on its website no later than thirty days prior to the next 14 election or day for registration, as applicable; provided that, in the 15 event of an unforeseen situation occurring on or after such thirty-day 16 period and rendering the whole or a substantial portion of a polling or 17 registration place inoperable for election purposes on the next election 18 day or registration deadline, as applicable, the board of elections 19 shall publish notice of the changed location of the polling or registra- 20 tion location on its website no later than the date after the day the 21 changed location is determined. 22 (c) The board of elections shall also transmit the notice made pursu- 23 ant to paragraph (a) of this subdivision by electronic mail, if such 24 information is available to the board of elections in the voter's regis- 25 tration information. Notice by electronic mail pursuant to this para- 26 graph shall not be deemed a substitute for written notice by mail. 27 § 2. This act shall take effect on the one hundred eightieth day after 28 it shall have become a law. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
