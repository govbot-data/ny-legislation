BYNOE -- read twice and ordered printed, and when printed to be committed to the Committee on Rules AN ACT to amend the general business law and the vehicle and traffic law, in relation to enacting the "vehicle streaming device ban" act The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The general business law is amended by adding a new section 2 392-l to read as follows: 3 § 392-l. Prohibit the sale of any device capable of allowing access 4 to streaming of video on the infotainment screen equipped to the center 5 console of such vehicle or any screen otherwise in direct view of the 6 driver of such vehicle while the vehicle is in motion. 1. For the 7 purposes of this section: 8 (a) "Streaming device" shall mean any device capable of allowing 9 access to streaming of video on the infotainment screen equipped to the 10 center console of such vehicle or any screen otherwise in direct view of 11 the driver of such vehicle while the vehicle is in motion. 12 (b) "Distributor" shall mean any person who delivers to a person other 13 than purchaser, for the purpose of retail sale. 14 (c) "Manufacturer" shall mean any person who makes and places into the 15 stream of commerce any device capable of allowing access to streaming of 16 video on the infotainment screen equipped to the center console of such 17 vehicle or any screen otherwise in direct view of the driver of such 18 vehicle while the vehicle is in motion. 19 (d) "Retailer" shall have the same meaning as set forth in subdivision 20 eleven of section four hundred ninety-a of this chapter. 21 (e) "Secondhand dealer" shall have the same meaning as set forth in 22 subdivision six of section four hundred ninety-a of this chapter. 23 (f) "Person" shall mean a natural person, firm, corporation, limited 24 liability company, association, or an employee or agent of a natural 25 person or an entity included in this definition. 26 2. No manufacturer, importer, distributor, wholesaler, retailer or 27 secondhand dealer shall sell, lease, offer for sale, or offer for lease 28 in this state any streaming device as defined in this section. Whenever there shall be a violation of subdivision two of this 9 section an application may be made by the attorney general in the name 10 of the people of the state of New York to a court or justice having 11 jurisdiction by a special proceeding to issue an injunction, and upon 12 notice to the defendant of not less than five days, to enjoin and 13 restrain the continuance of such violations; and if it shall appear to 14 the satisfaction of the court or justice that the defendant has, in 15 fact, violated this section, an injunction may be issued by the court or 16 justice, enjoining and restraining any further violations, without 17 requiring proof that any person has, in fact, been injured or damaged 18 thereby. Whenever the court shall determine that a 22 violation of subdivision two of this section has occurred, the court may 23 impose a civil penalty of not more than five hundred dollars for each 24 violation. Each sale of a streaming device in violation of this section 25 shall constitute a separate violation.
