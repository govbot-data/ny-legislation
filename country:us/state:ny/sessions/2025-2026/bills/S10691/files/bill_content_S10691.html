S10691 Summary:

BILL NOS10691
 
SAME ASSAME AS A11679
 
SPONSORCHAN
 
COSPNSR
 
MLTSPNSR
 
Amd §§125.25 & 70.00, Pen L
 
Enacts "Jacob Freytes' law"; provides that a person is guilty of murder in the second degree when they recklessly cause the death of a person less than eighteen years old; relates to sentences of imprisonment for felonies for causing the death of a person less than eighteen years old.
