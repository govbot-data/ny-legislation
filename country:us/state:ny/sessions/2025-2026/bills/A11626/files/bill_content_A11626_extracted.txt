Shrestha) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Science and Technology AN ACT to amend the general business law, in relation to the utilization of artificial intelligence The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. Short title. This act shall be known and may be cited as 2 the "options for search listing act". 3 § 2. The general business law is amended by adding a new section 399- 4 ss to read as follows: 5 § 399-ss. Artificial intelligence option. 1. As used in this section 6 the following terms shall have the following meanings: 7 (a) "Artificial intelligence" means a machine-based system that can, 8 for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommen- 9 dations, or decisions influencing real or virtual environments, and that 10 uses machine- and human-based inputs to perceive real and virtual envi- 11 ronments, abstract such perceptions into models through analysis in an 12 automated manner, and use model inference to formulate options for 13 information or action. 14 (b) "Search engine" means a website, software program, or system that 15 allows users to find indexed information on the internet. 16 (c) "AI search engine operator" means any person, partnership, associ- 17 ation, firm, or business entity, or any member, affiliate, subsidiary or 18 beneficial owner of any partnership, association, firm, or business 19 entity who operates for or provides a search engine with output gener- 20 ated at least partially by artificial intelligence. 21 2. AI search engine operators shall provide search engine users the 22 option to turn off any display of information which is generated by 23 artificial intelligence. 24 § 2. This act shall take effect on the one hundred twentieth day after 25 it shall have become a law. EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
