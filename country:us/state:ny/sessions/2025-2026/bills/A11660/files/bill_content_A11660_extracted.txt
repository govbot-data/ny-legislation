Powers) -- read once and referred to the Committee on Governmental Operations AN ACT to amend the public officers law, in relation to prohibiting certain employees of the state from using nonpublic information in prediction markets The People of the State of New York, represented in Senate and Assem- bly, do enact as follows: 1 Section 1. The public officers law is amended by adding a new section 2 74-b to read as follows: 3 § 74-b. Prediction markets prohibited. 1. For the purposes of this 4 section, the following terms shall have the following meanings: 5 (a) "New York state legislature" means the New York state assembly and 6 New York state senate. 7 (b) "Prediction market" means an exchange-traded platform or service 8 that is not licensed or permitted by the New York state gaming commis- 9 sion used by participants to buy and sell contracts contingent on future 10 events, such as elections, sporting events, actions or speech of indi- 11 viduals, and economic indicators. For the purposes of this section a 12 platform or service will not be considered a "prediction market" solely 13 because the platform or service is used to trade future contracts or 14 options to purchase or sell securities, commodities, or other goods or 15 services. 16 (c) "Public authority" means a public authority or public benefit 17 corporation created by or existing under any state law, at least one of 18 whose members is appointed by the governor, including any subsidiaries 19 of such public authority or public benefit corporation, other than an 20 interstate or international authority or public benefit corporation. 21 (d) "State agency" means any state agency, department, office, board, 22 commission or other instrumentality of the state, other than a public 23 authority. 24 2. No member of the New York state legislature or their respective 25 staff, no officer or employee of a state agency who serves at the pleas- 26 ure of the governor or their appointing authority, and no member of a 27 public authority appointed by the governor, shall use any nonpublic EXPLANATION--Matter in italics (underscored) is new; matter in brackets [ ] is old law to be omitted.
